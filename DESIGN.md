# Design System Normative Reference: Coffee Matte Finish Light Mode

## Visual Intent & Philosophy
The AEGIS Mission Operations Console uses an artisanal **Coffee Matte Finish Light Mode** calibrated for spaceflight human-action monitoring under bright command-center conditions.

1. **Anti-Glare Coffee Canvas**: Warm oat-milk cream matte background (`#f6f1ec`) prevents eye fatigue.
2. **Crisp Porcelain Crema Panels**: Solid white panels (`#ffffff`) framed with fine hairline coffee borders (`#ded2c5`).
3. **Deep Espresso Typography**: High-contrast dark roasted espresso (`#261912`) ensures maximum legibility (>15:1 contrast ratio) for procedure text, telemetry figures, and FSM states.
4. **Caramel & Bronze Accents**: Warm roasted caramel bronze (`#964f19`) anchors primary interactive actions and selected views.
5. **Tactical Contrast in Optics**: The camera viewport retains an enclosed, anti-reflective dark sensor enclosure (`#140f0c`) so real video frames and computer vision tracking reticles pop with precision.
